## Slide 7 — Weekly Brief Format
"The 6-section brief generated every week:
• This Week's Signal (one opinionated take)
• What Changed (specific metrics, not generic)
• Workflow to Redesign (with before/after + routing)
• Action This Week (3 specific verbs)
• Strategic Thesis Update
• Prediction to Track
